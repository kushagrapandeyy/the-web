## The rules behind a reservation

This high-school capstone explored how passengers and reservation staff could check availability, create bookings, retrieve tickets, and handle cancellations.

<span id="role-and-ownership" />
<span id="what-i-implemented" />

## My contribution

I built passenger and route records, availability checks, booking references, cancellation handling, and printable tickets.

<span id="architecture-and-decisions" />

## Architecture

**Python** implements reservation logic. **MySQL** models passengers, trains, routes, and bookings. **HTML/CSS** supports ticket entry and printing. Separating availability changes from presentation made the booking rules easier to reason about.

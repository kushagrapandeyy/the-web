## Equipment choices before checkout

Camera purchases often begin with a comparison of technical specifications. Student rentals add a different set of needs. This redesign gives both audiences a clearer path from finding equipment to completing their intended task.

<span id="role-and-ownership" />
<span id="what-i-implemented" />

## My contribution

I worked on research, wireframes, visual design, and an interactive Figma prototype. I designed category filters, product comparisons, cart and checkout screens, and account/student-rental flows.

<span id="architecture-and-decisions" />

## Design decisions

Product information leads the hierarchy. Comparison supports the decision before checkout, while rental and account paths are separated from ordinary purchases. Figma connects these screens into an interactive flow.

<span id="current-status" />
<span id="limitations-and-next-steps" />

## Status and next steps

The high-fidelity design prototype is complete. Next steps are testing with shoppers and student renters, then defining requirements for catalogue data, rental verification, accounts, and payments.

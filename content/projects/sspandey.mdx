## Document access beyond the office

A legal document workflow depended on the firm’s local network. Staff needed controlled access to the same legacy system while working remotely.

<span id="role-and-ownership" />
<span id="what-i-implemented" />

## My contribution

As a **Junior Developer**, I re-engineered document collaboration and retrieval into a Spring Boot service with JWT-secured REST APIs. This enabled remote access without requiring staff to stay on the LAN.

I also diagnosed repeated-request latency, worked on document-ingestion endpoints, and introduced caching across the hybrid cloud and on-premise environment.

<span id="architecture-and-decisions" />

## Architecture

**Java and Spring Boot** implement the service layer. **REST APIs** separate the application from the legacy storage workflow, and **JWT-based authorization** protects operations. Caching avoids repeated retrieval work.

I kept the existing document workflow and added a service layer around it. Staff could access the system remotely without replacing the underlying process.

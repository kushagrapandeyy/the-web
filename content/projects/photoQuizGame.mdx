## Making a word-search board readable

WordFinder explores the small decisions that make a mobile word-search game understandable: a readable grid, clear selection feedback, and a visible way to finish or replay a round.

<span id="role-and-ownership" />
<span id="what-i-implemented" />

## My contribution

I designed the layout and low-fidelity wireframes, including topic and difficulty choices, the game board, found-word feedback, score/timer presentation, and replay states.

<span id="architecture-and-decisions" />

## Design decisions

Figma links the interface states. Monospaced grid cells keep letters aligned, and selection contrast distinguishes found words without disrupting the board.

<span id="current-status" />
<span id="limitations-and-next-steps" />

## Status and next steps

The project is at the wireframe stage. The next step is a playable prototype with accessible input, followed by testing the selection gesture and game feedback.

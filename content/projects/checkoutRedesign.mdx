## Checkout without losing your place

A shopper should be able to change their cart without losing track of the order. This prototype focuses on recoverable edits and a clear view of payment and purchase details.

<span id="role-and-ownership" />
<span id="what-i-implemented" />

## My contribution

For an ASU HCI assignment, I designed a swipe-to-remove cart interaction with undo, a single-page checkout, payment-option presentation, a persistent order summary, and confirmation states.

<span id="architecture-and-decisions" />

## Design decisions

The Figma flow keeps the order summary visible during checkout. Undo makes item removal reversible, and payment choices are presented in context. Payment processing itself is outside the prototype.

<span id="current-status" />
<span id="limitations-and-next-steps" />

## Status and next steps

The Figma prototype is complete. Next steps are usability and accessibility testing, followed by engineering work on validation, payment integration, and failure states.

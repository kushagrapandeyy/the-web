## Bringing an Oudh brand to market

MSK Fragrance is an Oudh perfume brand in development. The work connects product identity and the online customer experience with practical questions about sourcing, pricing, and fulfillment.

<span id="role-and-ownership" />
<span id="what-i-implemented" />

## My contribution

As **co-founder**, I work on product development, brand identity, and the online experience. My contribution includes the initial web presentation and planning how product information, purchase, and fulfillment fit together.

<span id="architecture-and-decisions" />

## Product and launch decisions

At this stage, the important design decisions are product positioning, catalogue content, and the purchase journey. E-commerce and payment integrations belong to the launch plan, with implementation choices informed by sourcing and fulfillment requirements.

<span id="limitations-and-next-steps" />

## Status and next steps

The brand is **pre-launch**. Next steps are validating supply, pricing, and logistics, followed by testing the complete purchasing experience.

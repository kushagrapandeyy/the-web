## From matching to community

Lyra explores how a dating app can support the steps after an initial match. Guided onboarding, conversation, shared-interest circles, and events connect discovery with community and meeting in person, with safety considered throughout the experience.

## My contribution

I served as the **primary engineer** on a four-person ASU Computer Science capstone team, leading architecture and full-stack development and acting as the sponsor’s primary technical contact.

I built the React/TypeScript interface and FastAPI backend, implemented weighted matching, and integrated OpenAI for recommendations and the Guardian AI safety workflow. Unit tests, integration tests, and manual QA supported validation of the matching engines.

The Spring 2026 project, **CS/E-078**, was sponsored by **Jay Manwani of Endless Moments LLC**. Our team was Kushagra Pandey, Aditya Sharma, Erik Camacho, and Chaitanya Krishna Yadav Sulam.

<span id="architecture-and-decisions" />

## Architecture

**React and TypeScript** handle the interface. **FastAPI** exposes application operations and runs the Python matching logic. **PostgreSQL** stores preferences, match history, and conversation records. **Vercel** hosts the frontend; **Render** hosts the API.

Weighted scoring keeps matching criteria explicit and adjustable. AI-assisted recommendations complement that scoring; the safety workflow requires separate evaluation.

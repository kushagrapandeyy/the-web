## Finding the right book

I built this catalogue around a reader's path from browsing to selection: combine author, genre, price, and condition filters, inspect the results, and keep chosen books in a cart.

<span id="role-and-ownership" />
<span id="what-i-implemented" />

## My contribution

As the full-stack developer, I implemented faceted filtering, debounced search, cart state, paginated catalogue requests, input validation, and authenticated backend operations.

<span id="architecture-and-decisions" />

## Architecture

**React and TypeScript** manage the browsing interface and cart. **Node.js/Express** handles requests, with **Joi** for validation. **PostgreSQL** models books, editions, inventory, and orders; parameterized queries combine filters and indexes support common searches. JWTs protect authenticated operations.

Pagination bounds each catalogue response, while debouncing avoids sending a search request for every keystroke.

## Status and next steps

## Looking beyond total sales

Total sales can hide differences between regions and seasons. I used a Kaggle retail transaction dataset to investigate those patterns, then built a dashboard that lets users inspect the comparisons.

<span id="role-and-ownership" />
<span id="what-i-implemented" />

## My contribution

I handled the data preparation, statistical analysis, and frontend. I used regional z-tests and correlation analysis to explore seasonal patterns, then built chart interactions and filters for comparing periods and regions.

<span id="architecture-and-decisions" />

## Architecture

**Python, Pandas, and NumPy** support data cleaning and statistical analysis. **PostgreSQL** provides structured storage. **React** manages dashboard state, and **D3.js** renders interactive charts and tooltips.

Period and region filters let users examine how each slice of the dataset compares with the overall result.

## Software around the legal workflow

I founded **Firmlytic Solutions** in January 2024. The company is based in New Delhi, India. I am based in New York, NY. Its legal SaaS, **Sainya Mitra**, is in beta with 1,000+ registered cases across High Court and Supreme Court forums.

The beta supports case records, case tracking, and linked client accounts. Creating a case also sets up the linked account, connecting the firm's internal workflow with client access.

## From product decisions to deployment

As founder, I own product direction and lead engineering across the interface, backend services, and infrastructure. I work directly with stakeholders to understand the workflow, prioritize changes, and resolve issues during the beta.

### Sainya Mitra: legal platform in beta

My work covers the React interface, backend services, case-management workflow, and cloud deployment. I use unit and integration tests alongside manual QA to check changes before release, and maintain architecture and deployment documentation as the system evolves.

### AI-assisted document processing

I integrated the Claude API for document summaries, tagging, and clause extraction. The integrations are implemented; their usefulness and reliability in legal review remain under evaluation. They support the case workflow, with human review essential to interpreting the documents.

### OCR Scanflow: a separate prototype

I built **OCR Scanflow** with open-source OCR tools to explore a prospective client's document-processing workflow. It is a separate prototype within Firmlytic, intended to test the approach before deployment.

<span id="architecture-and-decisions" />

## How the system fits together

- **React** provides the application interface for working with case information.
- **Node.js and Hasura** support backend workflow logic and data access, respectively.
- **AWS** hosts backend infrastructure. Least-privilege IAM and Cloudflare WAF support access and perimeter controls.
- **Claude API** provides the document-analysis integration. Open-source OCR tools support the separate OCR Scanflow prototype.

The public company website shown above presents Firmlytic and its work. [Sainya Mitra](https://sainyamitra.in) is the legal application itself.

## What comes next

For Sainya Mitra, the next release depends on beta feedback, workflow reliability, access controls, and evaluation of AI output against real review needs. Precedent research remains exploratory.

For OCR Scanflow, the next step is prospective-client validation: testing extraction quality against representative documents and confirming how the prototype fits the intended process.

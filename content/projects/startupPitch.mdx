## Could advertising help fund a ride?

This ride-share concept asks whether physical advertising could help lower rider prices without reducing driver earnings.

<span id="role-and-ownership" />
<span id="what-i-implemented" />

## My contribution

As the solo designer and presenter, I developed the concept, pitch narrative, and Figma wireframes for onboarding and the core app flow. The deck connects the user problem to the proposed business model.

<span id="architecture-and-decisions" />

## Design decisions

Figma communicates the intended app experience; the pitch deck explains the advertising hypothesis. Driver, rider, and advertiser needs must fit together for the model to work.

<span id="current-status" />
<span id="limitations-and-next-steps" />

## Status and next steps

The pitch and concept prototype are complete. The business model remains a hypothesis. Interviews and a small prototype test would help assess demand and advertiser economics before investing in dispatch, mapping, or payments.
